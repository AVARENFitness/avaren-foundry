# Sprint 4.1D — Smarter Timed Movements

## Changed
- Timed movements can be completed early without being marked as skipped.
- Finish Early counts as completing the movement.
- Timer completion now presents one clear Continue button.
- Removed the duplicate Next Movement and Continue actions.
- Repeat Movement remains available as a secondary option.
- Skip Movement remains available only for rep-based movements.

## Preserved
- Adaptive Flow Composer
- Morning Movement and Daily Reset completion screens
- Saved duration preferences
- Timers, vibration, progress, and movement coaching
