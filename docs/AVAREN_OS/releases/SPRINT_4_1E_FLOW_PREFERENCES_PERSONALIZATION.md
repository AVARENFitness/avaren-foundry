# Sprint 4.1E — Flow Preferences & Personalization

## Added
- Short, Standard, and Extended routine-length preferences
- Routine length persists in cloud-synced app state
- Avoid This Movement control
- Disliked movements stay out of future Morning Movement and Daily Reset flows
- Recent completed flows are also considered when composing Morning Movement
- Completed flows save their movement IDs for future variety

## Behavior
- Short: 4 movements
- Standard: 6 movements
- Extended: 8 movements
- Length changes apply the next time a flow is opened
