# Sprint 6.6.1 — Assignment Lifecycle & Navigation Cleanup

## Assignment lifecycle
- Cancel removes an assignment from active schedules, athlete Home/Profile, and pending notifications while preserving history.
- Delete permanently removes an uncompleted assignment everywhere.
- Completed assignments are protected from permanent deletion.
- Existing cancelled assignments are cleaned out of calendar schedule rows and active notifications.
- Coach Calendar, client profiles, and Assignments include clear Cancel and Delete actions.

## Navigation cleanup
- Profile is divided into Overview, Training, Recovery, Account, and Support.
- Only one section is shown at a time.
- Important destinations remain one tap away without requiring a long page scroll.
- Notification badges remain visible on the Account section.
