# Sprint 6.6.2 — Assignment History & Builder Feedback

## Assignment history
- Cancelled assignments remain visible to the coach as history.
- Cancelled assignments include a permanent Delete action.
- Completed assignments remain protected.
- Active athlete and calendar views continue to exclude cancelled assignments.

## Workout builder feedback
- Adding an exercise immediately updates the count.
- A visible confirmation appears near the top of the builder.
- The builder scrolls directly to the newly added exercise.
- The new exercise is briefly highlighted.
- Custom exercises receive the same feedback.
