# Sprint 6.7.4 — Recipes, Batch Cooking & Meal Prep

## Added
- Ingredient-based recipe builder using the AVAREN food catalog and saved foods.
- Automatic total-batch and per-serving calorie and macro calculations.
- Batch yield and remaining-serving inventory.
- Fractional logging: quarter, third, half, one, one-and-a-half, two, or a custom serving amount.
- Duplicate recipe, prepare a new batch, and permanent recipe deletion.
- Every recipe remains inside the signed-in user’s isolated nutrition state.

## Product outcome
Users can build a homemade meal once, prepare a batch, and log portions in seconds without recalculating nutrition.
