# Sprint 6.7.5 — Nutrition Insights & Luxury UX

## Product
- Added a seven-day nutrition rhythm view for calories, protein, hydration, logging consistency, and weight change.
- Added one concise coaching insight instead of multiple competing alerts.
- Daily history is available through a disclosure rather than occupying the main screen.

## UX
- Consolidated Nutrition into Today, Meals, Library, and Insights.
- Goals remain one tap away from the header.
- Reduced competing card treatments and strengthened one-primary-action hierarchy.
- Added calmer spacing, quieter secondary surfaces, and bright-gym contrast.

## Quality
- Added a permanent product review checklist covering hierarchy, navigation, visual quality, interaction quality, product fit, and user isolation.
