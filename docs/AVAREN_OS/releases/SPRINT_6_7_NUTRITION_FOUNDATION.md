# Sprint 6.7 — Nutrition Foundation

## Added
- Standalone Nutrition tab for solo and coached users
- Daily calorie, protein, carbohydrate, fat, fiber, hydration, weight, and workout-calorie tracking
- Daily logs separated by local calendar date
- Saved foods and reusable recipes
- Batch recipe servings and fractional logging support through servings
- Water bottle and half-bottle shortcuts
- Nutrition goals and optional coach-access permission
- Daily history and Home nutrition summary
- Supabase nutrition profile/day schema with owner and optional connected-coach read policies

## Known limitations
- Food values are entered manually in this foundation release
- Natural-language logging, barcode lookup, label scanning, meal photos, and Apple Health integration are planned for later Nutrition Intelligence releases
