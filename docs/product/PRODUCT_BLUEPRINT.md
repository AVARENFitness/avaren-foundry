# AVAREN Product Blueprint

## Mission
AVAREN is the home for a person’s entire fitness life: training, nutrition, recovery, progress, and optional coaching in one premium experience.

## Primary users
- Solo athlete
- Coached athlete
- Coach

## Core pillars
1. Home — today at a glance
2. Train — workouts, programs, calendar, builder, history
3. Nutrition — food, macros, water, weight, recipes, goals
4. Progress — trends, PRs, weight, measurements, milestones
5. Account — settings, notifications, coach connection, support

Coach Hub is a separate role-based workspace and never clutters the solo athlete experience.

## Product rules
- Daily actions begin in two taps or fewer.
- Every screen has one obvious primary action.
- Important content appears before secondary detail.
- Long pages are divided into focused destinations.
- Readability must hold up under bright gym lighting.
- AI reduces manual work but never silently changes a plan.
- Every feature must work for solo users unless coaching is inherently required.

## Definition of done
A feature is complete only when it works, fits the navigation, matches the design system, is readable on mobile, handles empty/error/loading states, and passes solo and coached workflows.
